# Code of Conduct

Lastbook is built for people in hard moments. We want working on it to feel safe for everyone, whatever their
background, identity, experience or country.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticise work, not people. Say what is wrong and how it could be better.
- Welcome newcomers and questions. Nobody has to know everything already.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's personal details, home, location or private messages.
- Take care with safety. Advice on health, water, food or shelter can hurt someone if it is wrong, so cite your sources
  and say when you are unsure.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Discrimination, or slurs about anyone's identity or background.
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission.
- Pushing changes that would make Lastbook track, profile or expose the people who use it.

## Reporting

Email ferdous@hey.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers will look at every report, respond, and act
fairly. They may warn someone, remove content, or remove someone from the project.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere else someone represents the project.
